CopyCook started as a school project and turned into something we're genuinely proud of: a cozy little corner of the internet where food lovers can save, discover, and cook what they crave.

The origin

Why CopyCook?

Ever crave your favourite restaurant dish but wince at the price tag? CopyCook was built to fix that. It's a recipe discovery platform where you can find copycat recipes from popular chain restaurants and see exactly how much you save by making it at home instead.

With rising food prices and shrinkflation hitting everyone's wallets, we wanted to make home cooking feel accessible, affordable, and genuinely fun. Browse recipes, compare homemade costs against dine-in prices, save your favourites into personal folders, and build your own little cookbook, all in one cozy corner of the internet.
